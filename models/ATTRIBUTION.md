# Face detector

`face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx` is YuNet from the OpenCV Zoo (MIT License).
Source: https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet

# Speech recognition

`whisper-base/` (added by packaging/build.ps1, not kept in git) is OpenAI Whisper "base"
(MIT License, https://github.com/openai/whisper) converted for CTranslate2 by Systran
(MIT License, https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-base). Runs fully on the user's PC.
