MasterClip setup & help

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. The installer includes the processing tools and local speech model. You do not need to install Python or FFmpeg.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

This release is unsigned, so Windows may show an “Unknown publisher” or SmartScreen warning.

  1. Confirm you downloaded MasterClip from our official GitHub releases or our company download page.
  2. If the warning offers More info, open it and choose Run anyway only if you trust that source.
  3. Finish setup, then open MasterClip from the Start menu.

Some managed PCs and Windows Smart App Control settings block unsigned software without a “Run anyway” option. Email us if that happens; include a screenshot of the exact message.

New in 1.6: where to find things

Everything new is off until you use it, so your clips look the same as before.

  • Layouts and Set it manually: on the Design step (under the previews) and in the clip editor (✏️ Edit → Layout). Pick one of 11 layouts, or drag the coloured boxes over a frame of the stream: green = gameplay, orange = streamer cam, blue = an extra part. Pull a corner to resize, scroll on a box to zoom. Reset to auto puts the boxes back where MasterClip found them.
  • Cards, My brand, Outro & music: tabs next to Captions on the Design step and in the clip editor. Post cards (X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Breaking news), your social handles bar, logo, a background card around the video, an end card or your own outro video, and one song. Use music you are allowed to post.
  • Fix captions: clip editor → Words. Change words, split or join lines, move all captions earlier or later, import an SRT / VTT / ASS file, download SRT or VTT. How words move and My caption styles are on the Captions tab.
  • Use this on all clips of this video: the switch at the top of the clip editor. The whole look goes on every clip of that video (each keeps its own moment and title), and new clips of that video get it too.
  • Stars under each clip: after you rate 8 different moments, MasterClip leans to the kind of moments you rate high. Moment mix (Design step, settings): Balanced, Action or Talking.
  • 📜 Transcript and 🤖 Chatbot helper: buttons above your clips. Click the first and last word to make a clip of any part, or let ChatGPT / Gemini find moments in a long video and paste the answer back.
  • 📡 Creators (top bar): add a Twitch name or YouTube channel. While MasterClip is open it checks once a minute and clips the stream by itself when they go live (one stream at a time, 3 hours at most).
  • Phone remote (top bar → More): off until you turn it on. Open the address it shows on your phone (same Wi-Fi) and type the 6-digit PIN. Windows may ask to allow MasterClip on private networks.
  • 🖼 Cover on each clip makes a picture with the title, also used as the thumbnail when posting. Report a problem (bottom right) builds a report you copy or email; nothing is sent by itself.

Continuous clipping & automatic posting

  1. Choose Auto-post every clip. Paste your own Zernio key and connect the Instagram and/or YouTube accounts you want.
  2. Choose Clips per hour and your speed, or switch to daily times. Choose destinations: vertical clips can go to both; long videos go only to YouTube.
  3. Pick a live stream and Non-stop auto-clipping, choose a design, then start once. We scan each new minute and create clips of the best moments. No total clip target and no need to press Clip now.
  4. Finished clips appear with their posting status, scheduled time and Cancel post. Uploading to Zernio happens ahead of the scheduled publication time.
  5. Use New window (top bar → More) for another stream. Keep at least one MasterClip window and your PC running for capture, rendering and pending retries. Closing all windows stops local work; posts already scheduled on Zernio can still publish.

YouTube limit reached, Instagram still ready?

Each destination is independent. A YouTube limit pauses YouTube posts and leaves Instagram running. The app shows the provider's message and the next automatic retry time. If the provider does not state a reset time, the next check is not a guaranteed reset. Download a clip for manual upload if your platform account permits it.

HD / Full HD / 4K

For past streams and uploaded files, choose download and export quality in Design. 4K requests the highest available source up to 2160p and renders a 4K canvas. Lower-resolution footage and cropped regions cannot gain missing detail. Higher resolutions take more time and storage. Live capture uses a lighter setting for continuous recording.
